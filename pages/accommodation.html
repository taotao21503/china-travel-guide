🏨 Accommodation

How to choose, book and check into hotels in China
When planning a trip to China, one of the most important decisions you'll make is choosing the right hotel. With thousands of options available, it can be overwhelming to find a hotel that suits your preferences, budget, and comfort level. To help you navigate this process, we have compiled a comprehensive guide to choosing the perfect hotel in China. Whether you're a first-time traveler or a frequent visitor, this guide will equip you with the knowledge and tips to make an informed decision.

💭 Factors to Consider When Choosing a Hotel

You are recommended to book your hotel room before visiting China. Choose a trustworthy online hotel booking website, and choose a hotel and a room type based on your itinerary, budget and preferences. Alternatively, you may visit the official website of your preferred hotel brand to book your stay.

Please read instructions on the website or contact the hotel to make sure that the hotel can receive international visitors.

💰 Hotel Rates

China's hotel prices vary based on factors like lodging quality, location, and booking time.

CategoryPrice per Night (USD)
Budget (under 3-star)$30 – $50
Mid-range (3-star)$50 – $80
Luxury (4–5 star)$80 – $200+

Peak season: April to October — prices rise as visitors surge.

Slack season: November to March — prices often dip (exception: Chinese New Year holiday).

City price benchmarks for a standard room:

  • Beijing: ~USD 200
  • Shanghai: ~USD 180
  • Xi'an: ~USD 120
  • Guangzhou/Shenzhen: Similar to Shanghai
📋 Booking Options and Restrictions for Foreign Guests

Not all hotels in China are licensed to host foreign guests. Before making a reservation, ensure that the hotel is approved to accommodate international travelers.

Recommended booking platforms:

  • Trip.com (Ctrip) — English-language interface, supports international cards
  • Booking.com — Wide selection, flexible cancellation
  • Official hotel brand websites
  • Agoda, Expedia, etc.
🛄 Cancellation and Refund Policies

Before finalizing your booking, review the hotel's cancellation and refund policies. Unforeseen circumstances may require you to modify or cancel your reservation.

  • Some hotels offer flexible cancellation options (free cancellation up to 24–48 hours before check-in)
  • Others may have stricter policies (non-refundable rates are usually cheaper)
  • Consider travel insurance to cover unexpected cancellations
🔖 Hotel Facilities and Amenities

When researching hotels, pay attention to the amenities provided in the rooms, such as:

  • Air conditioning, free Wi-Fi, minibar, and a kettle
  • Private bathroom with toiletries, hairdryer, and hot shower
  • Non-smoking rooms or accessibility features if required
⚡ China provides a 220-volt, 50-Hz power supply. Appliances requiring 110-volt will need an adapter. Some higher-rated hotels provide these for free.
🚖 Additional Services and Features

Some hotels offer extra services that can enhance your stay:

  • Airport or train station transportation
  • Concierge services
  • Laundry facilities
  • On-site dining options
  • Business centre facilities
  • Childcare services
👛 Payment Methods at Hotels

The available payment methods at hotels include:

  • Cash (RMB)
  • Bank card (inquire in advance if Mastercard, Visa, or other international cards are accepted)
  • WeChat Pay and Alipay
  • Some hotels accept Apple Pay and PayPal
🚫 There's No Need to Tip — Tipping isn't an accepted practice in China, so there's no expectation for you to do so.
🧑‍💻 Hotel Reservations

Booking a hotel in China can be done through various channels, including online platforms like the Trip.com APP or via telephone.

While online booking is convenient and widely used, keep in mind that not all hotel staff members may speak English fluently. If you encounter any difficulties, consider working with a travel consultant who specializes in China.

It's advisable to book your hotel in advance, especially during peak travel seasons and holidays.

🧑‍💻 Checking-In at the Hotel

Checking into a hotel is easy and straightforward:

  1. Present your passport to a hotel staff member
  2. Confirm your reservation
  3. Fill out a registration form
  4. Receive your room card
🧳 Checking-Out of the Hotel

During check-out, a hotel staff member may inspect the room to ensure there are no damages or missing items. This process may take some time, so it's advisable to allocate extra time before your departure.

  • Return the room key
  • Retrieve the deposit receipt if applicable
  • Communicate any transport or luggage storage needs with staff before leaving
