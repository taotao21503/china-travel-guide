How to book tickets and visit China's attractions

🎫 How to Reserve Tickets in China?
  • International visitors are required to provide passport numbers during the online booking process.
  • Visitors must bring the original valid identification used to book their tickets for check-in.

Booking Platforms:

  • WeChat and Alipay official mini-programs
  • Third-party apps (Klook, Trip.com, GetYourGuide)
  • Official booking websites of specific attractions
👥 Can I buy a ticket at site instead of booking in advance?

Yes, you can. However, you may encounter several difficulties:

  • Language barrier: Many staff may not speak English at on-site ticket booths.
  • Long queues: Very common at popular attractions, especially during Chinese public holidays.
  • Limited tickets: Many scenic areas limit the number of on-site tickets sold per day — if you arrive late, tickets may not be available.
⚠️ We strongly recommend booking tickets online in advance to guarantee a smooth trip.
🖇️ How far in advance should I book a ticket?

After mapping out your China travel itinerary, you can start booking admission tickets:

  • Most scenic areas in China release tickets 7 days before the entry date.
  • Try to secure tickets as soon as they're available for popular attractions.
  • The latest you can usually book is the day before your intended visit.
💡 For top attractions like the Forbidden City in Beijing, tickets can sell out weeks in advance. Book as early as possible!
📲 How do I make a ticket order online and get a confirmation?
  1. Submit the order on the booking website/app and complete payment.
  2. Upon receiving your order, the platform will handle it immediately.
  3. A confirmation email will be sent to you.
  4. Click the "Check Order" link in the email to view your Confirmation Number or QR Code.
  5. Review the terms for "How to Use" in your confirmation.
📄 Do I need to redeem a paper ticket after booking online?

The process varies across different scenic areas:

  • Direct entry: Some scenic areas allow you to simply have your passport or QR code scanned at the gate — no paper ticket needed.
  • Paper ticket required: Some attractions require you to collect a paper ticket at the ticket window using your QR code, Confirmation Number, or passport.

Always check the specific instructions in your booking confirmation for the attraction you're visiting.
